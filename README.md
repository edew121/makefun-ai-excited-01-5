Entry for [make.fun ai-excited-01](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01) — "Make the AI judge excited".

A snow globe launches itself: on a deep-blue winter night, a little red-and-white rocket bursts out of its own glass dome, shattering the top as it climbs diagonally toward the viewer — glass shards, gold flame and speed lines flying. Inside the globe, a tiny village sleeps under warm yellow windows while a cratered moon watches from above.

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) draws the image procedurally and deterministically to `/output/entry.png` (1024×1024).
